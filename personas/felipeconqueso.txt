Steam vs Epic FAQ
The future of PC gaming will hinge on the outcome of the Steam vs Epic battle that will rage throughout 2019 and into 2020.  We the PC gamers are the foot soldiers in this conflict, and the stakes are high.  The choice is between a monolithic platform that encompasses all things PC gaming or a platform that pays publishers for exclusive titles that will split the gamer base between multiple platforms.  In order for us to make the proper choices we must first be informed.  For this reason I have assembled a FAQ regarding the Steam vs Epic debate.

&amp;#x200B;

What is causing all the outrage across PCMR about epic games?

Recently Epic Games began offering large payouts in order to secure timed exclusive rights to several games.  The list includes Metro: Exodus (with the exclusivity deal coming only weeks before launch), Borderlands 3, and Control.  Outer Worlds is not an Epic Games exclusive as it will also be available on the Microsoft Store.  In addition to the payout from the exclusivity deals Epic Games charges only 12% commission compared to Steam charging 30% initially with the rate decreasing to 20% after enough sales.

&amp;#x200B;

What does timed exclusive mean?

Timed exclusives ate games that will appear only on one platform for a certain amount of time, in this case most of the Epic exclusives are for 1 year.  After this time has passed the publisher is free to publish the game on other platforms.

&amp;#x200B;

Isn't it good that game developers are getting more from sales on the Epic Games store?

Game developers do not get the money from the sale of the game.  Most developers are contracted (or owned) by large companies called publishers.  The publishers pay an agreed upon fee to the developing studio for the game, and then the publisher markets and sells the game.  For example, Id Software made Doom 2016, but the game was published by Bethesda.  Sales profits went to Bethesda and not to Id Software.

&amp;#x200B;

Steam is almost a monopoly in PC gaming.  Epic is providing competition.  How can this be bad?

Competition is not bad.  It drives innovation.  What is happening here however is not competition.  Epic games is admitting that they cannot compete with Steam on the merits of their store and are instead paying millions of dollars to publishers in order for the games to be sold exclusively on Epic Games Store for a limited time.  Competition would be if Epic games began to roll out features that either Steam does not have, or are implemented better than the equivalent Steam feature.  This would serve as a reason for some users to switch to using Epic instead of Steam.  Steam would have to improve its platform, and each company would then be in war to outdo each other.  As it stands now it is only about who will throw the most money ate publishers.

&amp;#x200B;

You make the Epic Games Store sound bad.  Is it really that far behind Steam?

Unfortunately it is.  Features that Steam has, but are not implemented on Epic Games Store include game reviews, forums, workshop, multiplayer servers and anti-cheat for developers, marketplace, game streaming, and probably a few more I forgot.

&amp;#x200B;

You mentioned what Epic gets wrong, but what do they get right?

Epic Games Store is much more curated than Steam.  This means you will generally find higher quality games than the average Steam game.  Steam has allowed many low budget low quality asset flips to clutter the store, and it can be hard to find the games you want in the sea of mediocrity.  The Epic Games Store has much fewer fluff games.  Epic Games is also the creator of the popular Unreal 4 engine, and games sold on their store often use that platform.  The development teams have access to support for seamless integration into the Epic Games Store directly from epic themselves leading to better quality software integration.

&amp;#x200B;

Will Epic be able to implement all those missing features with the money they will make from exclusives?

Epic Games is partially owned by Tencent, a massive Chinese corporation.  Money is not an issue for them, and is likely not the reason these features have not been added.  However, Epic Games has said that they will be implementing many new features in 2019 in an effort to become a more competitive store platform.  It is likely that by the end of the year the Epic Games Launcher will be much more user friendly and have features that rival Steam.

&amp;#x200B;

Why not just use Steam for most of your gaming and then buy exclusives on Epic?  It's just another launcher, not a big deal.
