The result was amazing. Steering is still heavier than a regular family car, but it's noticeably lighter than before. Since PAS fluid is very similar to ATF I suspect this is related - these 'sealed for life' fluids are in fact perishable and Mazda may not have used the best quality fluids from factory. Changing the PAS fluid has made the steering lighter, but also somehow more accurate-feeling (the latter may be placebo but the former most certainly isn't!).

Wonderful when a cheap, easy mod makes such a dramatic difference.

A bit of an ashy aftertaste for my personal preference but totally passable, and head-and-shoulders above what I usually find with these supermarket gambles for, in percentage terms, a lot more money. This was passable morning brew for £1.89 a bag. I chalk that up as a win.

Now I'm back to enjoying a superb Union Brazilian blend ('Bobolink'), but after that, I'm taking another step into the competitively-priced field of war and trying Aldi's own, identically-priced offering.
